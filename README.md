# The Penthouse: War in Life

**The Penthouse** is my *favorite Korean drama series*. It’s full of revenge, mystery, and drama among the wealthy residents of **Hera Palace**.

> “The higher you climb, the harder you fall.”

---

## Main Characters
1. Shim Su-ryeon  
2. Cheon Seo-jin  
3. Oh Yoon-hee  
4. Joo Dan-tae  
5. Bae Ro-na  

---

### Why I Love It
- Strong acting  
- Great storyline  
- Full of twists  
- Beautiful set design  

---

`This show proves that money can’t buy happiness.`
